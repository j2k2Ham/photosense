# The iPhone app

- **The project:** plain .NET for iOS (not MAUI), bundle id `com.j2k2ham.trovolve`, iOS 16 or later.
- **The web view:** a `WKWebView` on `app://trovolve/`. `PageSchemeHandler` hands out `wwwroot` and sends `/api` to the router; on iOS a request's body is in `NSUrlRequest.Body`.
  - The page draws under the status bar and round the corners itself (`viewport-fit=cover`, `env(safe-area-inset-*)`), so the scroll view adjusts nothing. A swipe from the left edge goes back in the page's history (`AllowsBackForwardNavigationGestures`), closing what the page opened last.
  - The page tells the app its theme through the `trovolveBars` message handler, and the status bar's icons follow it (`PreferredStatusBarStyle`).
  - The app does not ask for photos as it starts: the page asks after Welcome (`library/ask`, `PhotoKitAccess.AskAsync`). iOS asks once only; after that only Settings changes it, and iOS restarts the app when it does.
  - The page knows it is in an app before it is drawn, by the `trovolveBars` handler (or Android's `TrovolveBars`), and stays hidden until it has its skin.
- **LiteDB compiles expressions while it runs,** which iOS allows only through .NET's interpreter, hence `<MtouchInterpreter>-all</MtouchInterpreter>`.
- **`PhotoKitLibrary`:**
  - Albums Trovolve made are known by the ids kept in `albums.txt` among the app's files. The person's own albums are never shown or changed.
  - Each `Change` is one `PHPhotoLibrary.PerformChangesAndWait`, so iOS asks once and makes all of it or none. Error 3072 means the person said no.
  - A folder made in the same block is filed with `objc_msgSend` and `addChildCollections:`, because the bindings take only collections that already exist.
- **Never delete an album:** iOS shows a question before an app deletes one, which hung the simulator's check. An emptied album stays.
- **Removed photos are not hidden:** with the Hidden album locked, the default since iOS 16, apps cannot see hidden photos at all.
- **`ImageIOAnalyzer`** measures pictures through the shared `ImageFingerprint`.
- **Compressing** (`PhotoKitLibrary.CompressAsync`): `AVAssetExportSession` on the asset from `PHImageManager.RequestAVAsset` (iCloud allowed), then a new asset by `PHAssetCreationRequest` with the original's date, place and favorite mark. iOS's presets choose the bitrate; there is no HEVC preset below 1080p, so Small is H.264 1280×720.
- **Notifications** (`IosNotifier.Shared`, the `UNUserNotificationCenter` delegate set in `FinishedLaunching`): a tap notes what it opens and tells the page (`trovolve-notice`). The low-storage check runs at launch, on `sceneDidBecomeActive:` (a scene app's delegate, not the app's, hears it) and in `BGAppRefreshTask` `com.j2k2ham.trovolve.storage-check`, which `Info.plist` must list, with the `fetch` background mode, or registering it fails.
- **Automatic backup with the app closed:** the `BGProcessingTask` `com.j2k2ham.trovolve.backup` (`processing` background mode), asked for by `AppDelegate.AskForBackupAsync` while automatic backup is on, runs `PhoneApp.BackUpInBackgroundAsync`, as Android's `BackupWorker` does, over Wi-Fi only. Nothing on the simulator starts it by itself; the debugger can (`e -l objc -- (void)[[BGTaskScheduler sharedScheduler] _simulateLaunchForTaskWithIdentifier:@"com.j2k2ham.trovolve.backup"]`). Never call that from the app: it is a private API.
- **`BonjourAdvertiser`** calls `DNSServiceRegister` from `libSystem` with no callback, and keeps the screen on while the phone offers photos.
- **`Info.plist`:**
  - `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`
  - `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`, and `NSBonjourServices` (`_trovolve._tcp`)
  - `PHPhotoLibraryPreventAutomaticLimitedAccessAlert`
  - `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption`, still false: an open question for Jamie, since the app now encrypts what it sends.
- **`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`:** App Store Connect refuses an upload without it.
- **`iphone.yml`:**
  - Pins Xcode 26.6, .NET SDK 10.0.401 and the iOS workload set 10.0.401; each workload release needs one Xcode.
  - Makes a HEIC, an exact copy of it and a JPEG with `sips`, and adds them to the simulator's library.
  - Grants photo access, then rewrites the TCC row to 2/2/2, because simctl's grant reads as never asked on Xcode 26.
  - Launches the app with `TROVOLVE_SELF_CHECK=1` and waits for `self-check.json` to say `"finished":true`.
  - Its TestFlight job is skipped until the Apple account's secrets are set.
