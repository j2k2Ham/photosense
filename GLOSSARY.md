# Trovolve

Trovolve tidies a person's photos and videos on the device that holds them: it finds what is kept more than once, and sorts the rest by place and date.

## Language

**Capture**:
One moment the camera recorded: a single shot, whatever files it was later saved as.
_Avoid_: Shot (for the files), image

**Duplicate**:
A picture or video holding the same **Capture** as another, in any format, codec, size or quality. Clean up may remove duplicates in bulk, keeping one.
_Avoid_: Copy (for an **Edited version**), same picture (for two **Captures** that look alike)

**Similar shots**:
Files of different **Captures**, or a **Capture** and its **Edited version**, alike enough that the person chooses which to keep. Clean up never removes them in bulk.
_Avoid_: Near-duplicates, look-alikes

**Edited version**:
A **Capture** saved again after cropping, trimming or adjusting it. It and its original are **Similar shots**, not **Duplicates**.
_Avoid_: Edit copy, render

**Burst**:
A run of **Captures** taken in quick succession; its frames are **Similar shots**.
