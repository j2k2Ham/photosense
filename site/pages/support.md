# Support

{{name}} has a guide built in: choose Help in the menu. It explains every screen, and its search finds what you are looking for.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do the files I delete go?

Into a folder named `_PhotoSense_Removed`, inside the folder they came from, keeping their place within it. Nothing is erased. Undo, in the message that appears, puts them back.

### How do I erase them for good?

Choose Delete permanently in the menu. {{name}} says how many files are waiting and how much space they take, and asks before it erases anything. This cannot be undone.

### Does {{name}} upload my photos?

No. {{name}} works on your own device, and your photos and what it learns about them stay there. See the privacy policy.

### Why does {{name}} not show all my photos on my iPhone or iPad?

When {{name}} first asked to see your photos, you may have chosen to allow only some. The scan screen then says so: **Choose more photos** opens the phone's own picker, and **Open Settings** goes to where all of them can be allowed, under Privacy & Security, then Photos, then {{name}}.

### What is the difference between duplicates and similar shots?

Duplicates are copies of the same file. Similar shots are different pictures that look alike, such as a burst taken a moment apart. {{name}} suggests the best of each set to keep, and you decide.

### How do I remove {{name}} and everything it keeps?

On an iPhone or iPad, delete the app. On a computer, delete the program and {{name}}'s data folder for your user account. Files you removed but have not erased are still in the `_PhotoSense_Removed` folders.

## Writing to us

If the guide does not answer your question, write to {{supportEmail}}. To help us help you, please say:

- which device and system you use (for example, iPhone 17 with iOS 26, or Windows 11);
- the version of {{name}}, shown under About in the menu;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Please do not send photos unless we ask for one to look into a problem.
