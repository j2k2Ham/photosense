# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what {{name}} does with information about you and your photos. {{name}} is made by {{publisher}} ("we", "us"). If you have a question about it, write to {{supportEmail}}.

## In short

- {{name}} works on your own computer, iPhone or iPad. Your photos, and everything {{name}} learns about them, stay on that device.
- We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal information. {{name}} has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and sends no usage or crash data to us.
- The only times information leaves your device are when you choose to: when you look at a map, open a place in OpenStreetMap, or write to us. Each is described below.

## What {{name}} reads

On a computer, {{name}} reads the folders you choose. On an iPhone or iPad, it reads the photos and videos you allow when the system asks: all of them, only those you select, or none. You can change that at any time in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Photos.

{{name}} reads each file and the details stored inside it, such as when and where it was taken, the camera, and the size of the picture. It uses them to find copies and similar shots, to choose the best one to keep, and to sort pictures into folders by date and place.

## What {{name}} keeps, and where

To do its work, {{name}} keeps a record on the device it runs on: the names, folders, sizes and dates of the files it has looked at, a fingerprint of each picture used to compare them, the names of the places they were taken, small previews, the changes you have made (so that they can be undone), and your settings. This record never leaves the device.

- On a computer, it is kept in {{name}}'s own data folder for your user account. Deleting that folder deletes it.
- On an iPhone or iPad, it is kept inside the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Where your photos were taken

Many photos record where they were taken. {{name}} turns that position into the name of a nearby town or landmark using a list of place names built into the app, so it does not need to ask anyone. {{name}} never uses the current location of your device.

## Maps

Where {{name}} shows a map, the map comes from Google Maps. To draw it, your device asks Google for the part of the map being shown, which tells Google that area (which may be where your photos were taken) and your device's internet address. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy, at https://policies.google.com/privacy, and its terms for Google Maps, at https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/. {{name}} places your photos on the map itself; it does not send Google the photos or their list of positions. If you do not open a map, nothing is sent to Google.

{{name}} for iPhone and iPad does not show maps.

## Opening a place in OpenStreetMap

If you choose to open a photo's place in OpenStreetMap, your browser goes to openstreetmap.org with that position, and OpenStreetMap receives it under its own privacy policy, at https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. Nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## What we receive

We do not receive your photos, their details, the record {{name}} keeps, or anything about how you use it. {{name}} does not use cookies or any similar way of recognising you. Its settings are kept in its own storage on your device.

If you got {{name}} from Apple's App Store, Apple may share with us statistics about downloads and sales, and crash reports and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device's settings. Apple gives us these without your name or contact details, and handles them under its own privacy policy, at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## Purchases

If {{name}} offers anything for sale, it is sold through the store you got {{name}} from, which takes the payment. We do not receive your payment details. On the App Store, Apple handles purchases under its own terms and privacy policy, and tells the app only what has been bought, so that it can be unlocked.

## When you write to us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you, keep them only as long as that takes, and never sell or share them. Please do not send photos unless we ask for one to look into a problem.

## Children

{{name}} is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the European Union's and United Kingdom's General Data Protection Regulation or California's Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal information held about you, such as the rights to see it, correct it, have it deleted, and object to its use. Because we do not collect personal information, apart from the messages you send us, there is usually nothing held about you to see or delete. You can ask us at any time what we hold and to delete your messages, by writing to {{supportEmail}}. We do not sell or share personal information, as those laws define it.

## Security

The record {{name}} keeps stays on your device, protected by that device's own security, such as its passcode and disk encryption.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted where you found this one, with a new date at the top. If a change affects what {{name}} does with your information, we will also say so in the app before it takes effect.
